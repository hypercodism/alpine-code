# Writing

Words are most of what people read in a product. Write every word in the person's language; for Korean, write like
토스: 해요체, short, plain, one message per sentence.

## 토스's eight writing principles

| Principle | Means | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Predictable hint | the words tell what happens next | 혜택 더 받기 | 설문에 답하면 쿠폰을 드려요 |
| Weed cutting | cut words that change nothing | 앞으로 자동이체를 설정할 수 있습니다 | 자동이체를 설정할 수 있어요 |
| Remove empty sentences | no sentence that only fills space or repeats the title | 예약 완료 / 예약이 완료되었습니다. 예약이 정상적으로 처리되었습니다. | 예약했어요 / 10월 25일 오후 3시에 만나요 |
| Focus on key message | only what is needed to act now | a paragraph about every option | 직접 고르거나, 가장 빠른 시간으로 잡을 수 있어요 |
| Easy to speak | reads naturally aloud, no stiff written forms | 본 상품의 수령 일자를 확인하시기 바랍니다 | 받는 날을 확인해 주세요 |
| Suggest over force | inform and let them choose; no fear | 지금 안 하면 혜택이 사라져요! | 원할 때 시작할 수 있어요 |
| Universal words | no slang, memes or insider terms | 이건 완전 꿀템 | 많이 찾는 빵이에요 |
| Find hidden emotion | think how the person feels reading it | 주문이 취소되었습니다 | 주문을 취소했어요. 결제한 돈은 2-3일 안에 돌아가요 |

## Rules of thumb

- 해요체 everywhere ("~해요", "~할까요?"); not 하십시오체 ("~하십시오", "~됩니다") and not 반말, unless the concept
  is a playful brand that speaks that way on every screen.
- Active and personal: "주문을 받았어요", not "주문이 접수되었습니다".
- Buttons are verbs of what happens: "예약하기", "담기", "보내기"; not "확인", "다음", "OK" when a verb fits.
  A confirm dialog's button repeats the action ("삭제하기"), the other is "취소".
- Titles say the thing, not the screen's name: "오늘 나온 빵 12가지", not "상품 목록".
- Numbers and specifics over adjectives: "3분 안에 끝나요", not "간편하게".
- Errors: what happened, then what to do. "인터넷 연결이 끊겼어요. 연결되면 다시 시도해 주세요."
- Empty states invite: "아직 저장한 글이 없어요" + the action.
- Ask with a suggested answer: "몇 시에 받으시겠어요?" with the earliest time already picked.
- Avoid foreign words when a Korean one is plain (로그인 is fine; 인게이지먼트 is not). Avoid Sino-Korean office words
  (금일, 익일, 상기, 해당) in favour of 오늘, 내일, 위, 이.
- Sentence length: one line on a phone when you can, two at most for a body paragraph.
- No exclamation marks in system messages; at most one in a celebration.

## Example content

Example data on screens is part of the design. Use real-sounding Korean names (김서연, 박지훈), real places (성수동,
연남동), plausible prices (4,500원), relative dates (오늘, 어제, 10월 28일), and enough items to read as real (four to
eight). Match the concept: a bakery's items are 소금빵 and 무화과 깜빠뉴, not "상품 1".
